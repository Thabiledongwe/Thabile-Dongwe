## How I set an appointment
1. **Warm opening:** greet by name, state the reason for the call
2. **Ask first:** understand what the patient needs before offering times
3. **Offer two options:** two specific times beat "when are you free?"
4. **Confirm the next step:** date, time, how the visit works, what to prepare
5. **Document:** record the outcome and next action in the CRM
6. **Remind:** confirm two days before, with an easy way to reschedule
